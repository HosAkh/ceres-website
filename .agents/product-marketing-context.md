# Ceres Technology — Product & Marketing Context

## What Ceres Does

Ceres Technology is an enterprise AI supply chain intelligence platform. It connects internal ERP and planning data to live external signals (weather, commodity prices, geopolitics, trade policy, port activity) so supply chain and commercial teams can see disruptions before they hit.

Core value proposition: **predictive, external signal intelligence layered onto your existing ERP** — not a replacement, an intelligence layer.

## Target Customers

- **Pharmaceutical companies** (Tier 1 CDMOs, API manufacturers, branded and generic pharma): drug shortage prevention, raw material risk, regulatory country-of-origin compliance
- **Hospitals & Health Systems**: formulary risk scoring, supplier disruption early warning, tariff/HTS compliance, multi-tier visibility
- **Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG)**: forecast reconciliation, seasonal signal monitoring (monsoon/weather), commodity input cost tracking, over-forecast detection
- **Investment Firms**: external signal enrichment for due diligence, macro overlay, exposure mapping, model enrichment

## Brand Voice & Tone

- Enterprise, confident, direct — not hype-y
- Speaks to supply chain practitioners and commercial planners, not just executives
- Avoids buzzwords: no "revolutionize", "transform", "seamless", "next-gen"
- Uses concrete, specific language: "3 weeks earlier", "SKU-level", "formulary", "HTS code"
- Second person: "your", "you"
- Honest — no invented stats or fabricated case studies

2. 35,000+ global indicators
3. Multi-tier supplier visibility (beyond Tier 1)
4. Explainable outputs — shows the signal behind every recommendation
