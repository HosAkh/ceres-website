Internal models are not enough on their own

Investment teams spend countless hours building proprietary models, yet these models often exist in a vacuum. Reconciling internal views with external realities—shifting macroeconomic conditions, geopolitical unrest, infrastructure changes, and evolving demographic trends—is a manual, error-prone bottleneck.

Without continuous, structured integration of these external signals, risk analysis remains reactive, and due diligence is constrained by the limits of human processing power and siloed spreadsheets.
