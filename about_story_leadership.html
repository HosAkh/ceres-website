history

Built from a real operating problem

Ceres was born out of sheer frustration. Founders Bobby Katoli and Trey Hoobler were managing complex coffee supply chains when the COVID-19 pandemic hit.

They quickly realized that while they had plenty of internal data—ERP records, spreadsheets, historical logs—they had zero connected context regarding real-world disruptions affecting their shipments.

The realization was simple but profound: businesses don't lack records; they lack the situational awareness to act on those records before it's too late.
